About Aspire

Technology should serve people, not the other way around.

I'm Richard Asp, and I founded Aspire Consulting Partners to help small businesses navigate the increasingly complex world of technology — particularly AI — without the hype or confusion.

With decades of experience, including founding AOSoft.com and spending 9+ years at cPanel, I've seen technology transform businesses from the inside out. Today, I focus on practical AI implementation, web hosting guidance, and digital systems that actually work for real-world businesses.

My approach is simple: understand your needs, recommend solutions that fit your budget and goals, and provide ongoing support without the corporate overhead. Whether you're exploring AI for the first time or need guidance on hosting infrastructure, I bring hands-on experience and honest advice.
