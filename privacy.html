Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Aspire Consulting Partners LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at aspireconsultingpartners.com or engage with our consulting services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and business or organization name that you voluntarily provide through our contact form or direct communication.
  • Communication Data: The content of messages you send us through the contact form, email, or other channels, including your preferred contact method.
  • Usage Data: General information about how you interact with our website, such as pages visited and time spent on the site. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Business Information: Details about your business, projects, or technical needs that you share with us during consultations.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the consulting services you request
  • To communicate with you about our services, including follow-ups and project updates
  • To improve our website and the services we offer
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our legitimate business interests

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share information with trusted third-party tools and platforms that help us operate our business (e.g., email services), subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With Your Consent: We may share information when you have given us explicit permission to do so.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, both of which implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Contact form submissions and communication records are retained for the duration of our business relationship and a reasonable period thereafter.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Opt-Out: You may opt out of receiving communications from us at any time by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
